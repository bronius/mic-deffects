What is this?!

Mic Deffects plays your voice back in near-realtime with a twist: parts of it come out reversed. Tap Start, allow mic access, and just talk.

Don't miss the Tuning section below the meter — three sliders that change how the effect sounds, from subtle (only specific sounds reverse) to constant granular glitchiness. Worth playing with.

Headphones work best, to avoid mic/speaker feedback.

Once you're listening, hit ● REC to capture up to 15 seconds — stopping it also stops the live session, and you can ▶ Play it back right after.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No audio leaves your device.
